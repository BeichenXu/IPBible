## Sources and changes

- Spring character sheets: [Updated Spring Characters](https://studio.blender.org/blog/updated-spring-characters/). Spring, Autumn and Alpha retain the original compositions and embedded credits. The fourth image is the project's official film still / page header.
- Sintel: [official gallery](https://durian.blender.org/gallery/). The selected art includes the character concept, face-model development, adult dragon study and mountain-city concept. Early development images are identified as such, not asserted to be final canon.
- Big Buck Bunny: [official media gallery](https://peach.blender.org/media-gallery/). The three images are official movie stills.

All images were proportionally resized to a maximum dimension of 1600 px and encoded as WebP (quality 90). No cropping, repainting, background removal or generative editing was performed. Original and delivered SHA-256 hashes, exact source URLs, dimensions and credits are recorded in `manifest.json`; per-image attribution is also carried in `catalog.json` so projects and exports can retain it. Descriptions are short Chinese summaries for the examples.

Preserve the corresponding credit and license link when sharing or exporting these artworks. Embedded logos remain part of the source images; no separate logo assets, trademark permission or affiliation is granted.

## Content boundaries

These samples do not include an official map or faction-territory data. Do not present generated geography, organizations or relationships as the original IP's canon. A user may add their own authorized map and setting text.

Pepper&Carrot is not bundled: its current official site explicitly states “AI derivation prohibited”; another creator's assets should not become an implicit AI-processing default. The 3 Blender projects above have separately verified open-content terms.
